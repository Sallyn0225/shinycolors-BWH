# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Shiny Colors fans checking B/W/H stats for curiosity, discussion, or a quick lookup. They arrive knowing the cast or at least a few names, and want a trustworthy number without wading through wiki layout or fan-score noise.

## Product Purpose

A small static fan tool for the 28 idols in 偶像大师 闪耀色彩: browse raw bust / waist / hip values in cm, rank the roster or unit averages on one metric, and compare two members side by side. Success is finding the right figure quickly and trusting that it is original source data, not a derived score.

## Positioning

**Raw centimeters only** — B, W, and H as published values, with no composite index, beauty rank, or judgment language.

## Brand Personality

**Bright, playful, fan-friendly.** Light idol-game energy that stays useful and warm, without tipping into merch push or official-store gloss. Voice stays clear and direct; numbers lead, personality rides alongside.

## Anti-references

Not a beauty ranking or thirst-bait scoreboard. No objectifying leaderboards, hot-or-not framing, or copy that evaluates bodies.

## Design Principles

1. **Numbers first, attitude second.** Every screen should make the cm value the easiest thing to read; personality never buries the data.
2. **No judgment by design.** Rankings are sort order on a single raw metric, never an implied score of worth.
3. **Short path to the answer.** Filter, search, and compare should resolve a lookup in a few interactions.
4. **Fan-tool honesty.** Non-official, source-linked, transparent about what the page will and will not claim.
5. **Playful without pressure.** Color and unit identity can feel bright; the UI still prioritizes calm scanning over spectacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Solid defaults: visible keyboard focus, readable contrast for body and UI text, and respect for reduced motion. Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA where practical; fix contrast and labeling gaps as part of normal polish, not as an afterthought.
